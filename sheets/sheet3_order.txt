Room 12 class supply list (invented: invented teacher, invented room parent, invented list; the stores and products it is checked against are real).

Written by Ms. Alvarez, 3rd-grade teacher. Date 2026-11-20. The room parent, Dev, buys one set for each of the 24 students with the class fund. Class fund purchases need my signature before anything is bought.

The list has four things, one of each for every student.
1. Headphones, for computer time. They must play no louder than 85 decibels. If a page states a louder level or mode, the loudest one counts.
2. Crayons, for art. A box must have exactly 24 crayons.
3. Notebook, for writing. It must have at least 70 sheets. 100 sheets if they have them.
4. Pencils, for writing. A pack must have at least 12 pencils.

I wrote what each thing must be, not which brand to buy. The person buying cannot change the list. A rule is only changed by me, and only my signature releases a purchase from the class fund. A product whose page does not state the figure is not bought on a guess. Nothing is checked out or paid for by the shopping agent.

In short, our order requires:
Our order requires headphones for computer time.
Our order requires crayons for art.
Our order requires a notebook for writing.
Our order requires pencils for writing.
