House rules for what Leo may buy for himself (invented: invented parent, invented teenager, invented rules; the stores and products they are checked against are real).

Written by Maya, Leo's parent. Date 2026-11-20. Leo chooses what he wants. I pay, and nothing is bought until I sign.

There are three things on Leo's list.
1. Headphones, for school. They must play no louder than 85 decibels. If a page states a louder mode or setting, the loudest one counts.
2. Electric scooter, for getting to school. Its top speed must be no faster than 15 miles per hour.
3. Energy drink, for practice. It must have at most 100 milligrams of caffeine in a can.

These are my rules. Leo cannot change them, and neither can anything he tells his shopping agent. A rule is only changed by me, and only my signature releases a purchase. A product whose page does not state the figure is not bought on a guess. Nothing is checked out or paid for by the shopping agent.
