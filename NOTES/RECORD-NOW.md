## If take C goes wrong
- **Before Send to Dana:** nothing has changed. Click **Close**, stop, start again.
- **The ask does not reach the phone in 5 s:** keep recording. Phone > **Adjust a limit** > drag "shower chair holds at least" to 375. The captions stay true.
- **Wrong figure sent:** phone > **Decline**, then ask again. Accepted but not signed and you want the beat again: phone > **Adjust a limit** back to 300 (no reset needed), start again.
- **"identity not checked" under Sign:** `curl -X POST localhost:8800/api/admin/persona/simulate -H 'content-type: application/json' -d '{"action":"approve"}'`, refresh the phone, go on in the same take; the gap is cut out.
- **The phone cannot be reached or mirrored:** the 390 px window (`open -na "Google Chrome" --args --user-data-dir=/tmp/signoff-phone --app="http://localhost:8800/phone/" --window-size=390,872 --window-position=1338,94`). It is a new "phone": enrol and check identity in it first.
- **After the second tap, whatever happened: do not reset.** A clumsy take is trimmed with its in and out times. A cart reading "pending": hold ten more seconds and change caption C4 to "Signed by Dana. One store has not answered yet. Nothing is checked out." If the recording was lost: record 15 s of the signed screen (step 6 "signed by Dana", step 7, the cart links) with the phone beside it, use it for the last two C lines with the caption "Dana signed on her phone a minute ago. Carts at the real stores. Nothing is checked out.", and delete the first two C lines.
