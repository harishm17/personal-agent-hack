Equipment handout: getting the bathroom and bedroom ready for a hip surgery recovery (invented handout for this demonstration; it stands in for a published patient handout).

Check the label or the product page for the weight capacity before buying any seat, chair, bar or rail. Do not use equipment above its stated weight capacity.
A raised toilet seat adds height to the toilet. Add the height the seat adds to the height of the toilet rim to get the finished seat height.
A shower chair or transfer bench must fit inside the tub. Measure the inside width of the tub and compare it with the overall width of the chair, not its seat width.
A walker must pass through the bathroom door. Measure the clear door opening and compare it with the overall width of the walker.
A bed rail must fit the thickness of the mattress.
A grab bar is screwed into a wall stud or a wall anchor rated for it and is fitted by a person who can do that. A suction bar is not a grab bar.
If a product page does not state a figure, do not guess it. Ask the store, or choose another product.
Some stores do not accept returns on bath seats once opened. Read the store's return terms before buying.
